Frequently asked questions

The questions we get asked most, answered straight. If yours isn't here, text us a photo and ask — that's the fastest way to get a real answer.

How do I get a quote?
Text us a photo of the job to 0423 325 989. We're usually out of reception while we're working, so texting is the fastest way to reach us — we'll get back to you the same day with a quote.
What areas do you cover?
We're based in Kangaroo Valley and cover the wider Shoalhaven, including Beaumont, Berry, Bomaderry, Sussex Inlet, Vincentia, Basin View, St Georges Basin and Berrara. See our service areas page for the full list.
Do you do one-off jobs, or only ongoing maintenance?
Both. We do one-off clean-ups, pre-sale tidy-ups and overgrown rescues, as well as scheduled mowing and maintenance programs.
Are you insured?
Yes, fully insured. We'll talk you through the work before we start, so there are no surprises.
How do you price a job?
Every job gets quoted on its own. We look at the actual work rather than apply a standard rate card, because no two gardens or properties are the same. Text a photo and we'll give you a straight answer.
Can you take on a garden that's badly overgrown?
Yes — property clean-ups and garden makeovers are one of the services we do most. Send a photo so we can see what we're dealing with.
Do you only do lawns, or bigger landscaping work too?
Both. Lawn mowing and garden maintenance sit alongside design, retaining walls and paving, irrigation and drainage, pond work and stump grinding — we cover the full range rather than just mowing.
What if I need something done urgently?
Text a photo and let us know it's urgent — we'll fit it in where we can, but we're usually booked ahead, so the earlier you reach out the better.

Got a garden job you want sorted?

Text us a photo of the place and we'll get back to you the same day with a quote. Every job gets quoted on its own.
