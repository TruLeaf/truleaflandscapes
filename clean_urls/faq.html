Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our landscaping, garden maintenance, and lawn care services in Kangaroo Valley and the Shoalhaven.

What areas of NSW do you service?

We service the Kangaroo Valley region and surrounding areas including Nowra, Bomaderry, Berry, Merton, Sussex Inlet, St Georges Basin, Crescent Head, and the wider Shoalhaven region.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. We provide free, no-obligation quotes for all our services. Simply text us a photo of your garden, or give us a call, and we'll arrange a consultation within 24 hours.

Do I need to be home during the work?

No. We can complete most services while you're out. However, for complex projects we recommend being available for the initial consultation to discuss your requirements.

What is your turnaround time?

For lawn mowing and regular maintenance, we typically complete jobs within 24 hours of booking. For larger projects like garden makeovers and landscaping, we'll discuss a timeline during the consultation.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance contracts?

Yes. We offer flexible recurring maintenance plans for lawns, gardens, and properties. Contact us to discuss a plan that suits your needs and budget.

Are your workers insured?

Yes. All our workers are fully insured and trained. We carry public liability insurance and every team member is screened and trained for the work they perform.

Do you handle garden waste removal?

Yes. We remove green waste, brush, and demolition waste from most sites. We'll advise on any special requirements when you book your quote.

Can you work around existing trees and plants?

Absolutely. We take special care around established trees and native plants. We'll always discuss your specific requirements during the consultation.

Do you provide garden design services?

Yes. We offer full garden design services, from concept plans to implementation. We'll work with you to create a design that suits your space, lifestyle, and budget.

What is your service area?

We service the Kangaroo Valley region and surrounding areas including Nowra, Bomaderry, Berry, Merton, Sussex Inlet, St Georges Basin, Crescent Head, and the wider Shoalhaven region.

Can you help with a property makeover?

Yes. We regularly complete property makeovers and full garden transformations. We'll work with you to create a plan that transforms your outdoor space.

What is your policy on garden cleanup?

We provide thorough garden cleanup services, removing all green waste and clutter to leave your property looking clean and fresh.

Do you offer weekend work?

Yes, we're flexible with scheduling and can work weekends to fit around your availability.

What should I do to prepare my garden for a makeover?

Clear the area of large items, note any specific plants you want to keep or remove, and have a plan of what you'd like to achieve. We'll handle the rest.

Can you help with a new home garden?

Yes. We can help with garden design, planting, and ongoing maintenance for new homes. Contact us for a consultation.

Do you offer commercial property maintenance?

Yes. We offer commercial property maintenance and landscaping services for businesses of all sizes.

How do I book a service?

Simply text us a photo of what you need, call us on 0423 325 989, or use our contact form. We'll get back to you within 24 hours.

Do you work with elderly or disabled clients?

Yes. We're experienced in providing care and maintenance support for elderly and disabled clients. We'll work at your pace and accommodate your needs.

Still have questions?

We're here to help. Text a photo or call us and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
