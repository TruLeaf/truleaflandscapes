Evergreen Plants for Shoalhaven Gardens

By TruLeaf Landscapes  ·  4 min read

Evergreen plants are the backbone of a healthy, year-round garden. In Kangaroo Valley and the Shoalhaven, they provide essential year-round structure, shade, and habitat for local wildlife. Here is what to plant for a vibrant evergreen garden that thrives in our climate.

Why Evergreen Plants Matter

Evergreen plants provide structure and continuity in a garden. While deciduous plants lose their leaves in winter, evergreens keep their foliage, ensuring your garden looks good all year. They also provide year-round shade, wind protection, and habitat for native wildlife.

Native Evergreens for the Shoalhaven

Native plants are well-adapted to the local climate and soil. Good choices include Westringia (coastal rosemary), Banksia, Grevillea, Lemon Scented Tea Tree, and Callistemon (bottlebrush). These plants are drought-tolerant once established and provide food and habitat for native wildlife.

Exotic Evergreens for Structure

Exotic evergreens like Lavandula, Rosmarinus officinalis (rosemary), Buxus, and Pittosporum provide structure and form. They are ideal for hedging, topiary, and garden borders. Many are drought-tolerant and low-maintenance once established.

Tips for Planting Evergreens

Plant in well-drained soil, mulch heavily to retain moisture, and water regularly until established. Group plants with similar water and light requirements together. Ensure good air circulation to prevent fungal issues.
