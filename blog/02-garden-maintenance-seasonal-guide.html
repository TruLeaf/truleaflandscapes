Seasonal Garden Maintenance Guide for Kangaroo Valley

By TruLeaf Landscapes  ·  4 min read

Gardens in Kangaroo Valley and the Shoalhaven require care that is tailored to our local climate. With warm summers, mild winters, and variable rainfall, a seasonal approach keeps your garden healthy and thriving. Here is what to do each season.

Summer (Dec – Feb)

Summer is about keeping your garden alive. Regular mowing at the right height, deep watering in the early morning, and mulching to retain soil moisture are essential. Watch for pests and diseases that thrive in the heat. Prune any heat-stressed plants and keep pathways clear of leaves and debris.

Autumn (Mar – May)

Autumn is the ideal time for planting, mulching, and preparing for winter. As temperatures cool, grasses recover from summer stress. Fertilise to promote root growth, and tidy up fallen leaves. It is also the time to plan for spring – consider what to plant or upgrade.

Winter (Jun – Aug)

Winter is a maintenance season. Grass growth slows, so mowing is less frequent, but heat and pest control remain important. Weeding is easier in winter when weeds are visible. Prune deciduous plants while dormant to encourage healthy spring growth.

Spring (Sep – Nov)

Spring is the most active growing season. Activate your garden with a balanced fertiliser, re-mulch beds, and plant new additions. Regular mowing resumes and is key to keeping your lawn dense and healthy. Watch for pests and diseases as temperatures rise.
