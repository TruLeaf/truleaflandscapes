Meet Mathew

I'm Mathew McLean, and I run TruLeaf Landscapes out of Kangaroo Valley. I've spent 18+ years in horticulture and landscaping, across the Gold Coast, the Mid North Coast and now here.

I started TruLeaf because I wanted to work with gardens, not against them. Permaculture taught me to look at what a place wants to be — the soil, the water, the aspect, the plants that already want to grow there.

That's the work I love. I'd rather plant something that's still thriving for you in ten years than something that looks good for a week. And I'd rather quote you honestly for what a job takes than pad it out.

If there's something outside what I do, I'll point you to a Kangaroo Valley tradesperson I know and trust to do it properly.

When I'm not working I'm in the garden, or out on the water. I care about how a place feels, not just how it looks on the day we finish.

We're fully insured, we turn up when we say we will, and we leave the place tidy.
