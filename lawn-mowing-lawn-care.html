What's included

  • Regular mowing — scheduled visits that keep the lawn tight and even through the growing season, so it never gets away from you.
  • Edging — clean lines along driveways, paths and garden beds, so the finish looks deliberate.
  • Clippings cleared — the lawn's left clean, not littered, and the paths and driveway are blown off.
  • Lawn health — we look at what's actually wrong before reaching for fertiliser, and we'll tell you if it needs something we don't carry. Often it's water or shade, not a product.
  • One-off or scheduled — a single tidy-up before a sale or an event, or a regular program all year.
